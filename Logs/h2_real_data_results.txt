INTERPRETATION (H2)
- Strong, highly significant POSITION BIAS: each extra displayed position multiplies the
  odds of selection by 0.607 (95% CI 0.591-0.623), i.e. -39% odds per position (p < 1e-290).
- No condition effect: Reverse OR=1.01 (p=0.91), Shuffled OR=1.00 (p=0.91) vs Ranked.
  All three conditions show nearly identical Spearman rho (~ -0.95): the position gradient
  dominates regardless of presentation order.
- model_weight has no independent effect once position is in the model (OR=1.18, p=0.91).
  CAVEAT: weight and position are perfectly collinear within Ranked/Reverse, so their
  effects cannot be separated there; only the Shuffled condition (r=-0.30) partially
  identifies the weight effect, and even there it is not significant (see Model 3).
- H2 conclusion: selection is driven by displayed position, not by model quality/weight;
  this pattern holds in all 3 conditions (rho ~ -0.95, all p < 1e-9).
